Clinical content last reviewed June 2026 · MSBOS: HFHS PCR-PALM-TRM-5.050 · ASFA 9th ed. (2023) · antigen frequencies: White donor population. Verify against current institutional policy before clinical use.

Created by Omar Z. Baba, MD Clinical Pathologist · Pathology Informaticist · Clinical Pathology Resident, PGY-III Department of Pathology & Laboratory Medicine · Henry Ford Health
Supervised by Rachel Brancamp, MD, MPA Associate Director, Transfusion Medicine Department of Pathology & Laboratory Medicine · Henry Ford Health

Educational and decision-support reference for a trained resident/physician — not a clinical protocol. The MSBOS is institution-specific (HFHS PCR-PALM-TRM-5.050); ASFA tables follow the 9th edition (2023); antigen frequencies are population estimates. Verify against current institutional policy and the source documents before acting on a patient; the attending / blood bank physician remains the final decision-maker.
